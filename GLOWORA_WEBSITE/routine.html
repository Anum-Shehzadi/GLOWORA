GLOWORA SKINCARE JOURNAL

Your Simple Guide to a Healthy-Looking Glow

A good routine doesn't need to be complicated. Start with the basics, introduce products gradually, and pay attention to how your skin feels.

EXPLORE SKINCARE →

THE ESSENTIALS

Build Your Routine in 4 Steps

Keep your routine consistent and choose products based on your skin's needs, not the number of steps.

01

Cleanse

Use a gentle cleanser to remove excess oil, sweat, sunscreen, and daily buildup without leaving your skin feeling stripped.

02

Treat

If needed, apply one targeted product—such as a serum—following its label directions. Introduce new actives one at a time.

03

Moisturize

Apply a moisturizer to help keep skin comfortable and support its moisture barrier. Choose a texture that suits your skin.

04

Protect

In the morning, finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher. Reapply as directed, especially outdoors.

START YOUR DAY

Morning Routine

  1. CleanserBegin with a gentle cleanse, or rinse with water if that suits your skin.
  2. Optional serumChoose one targeted product if you have a specific skin concern.
  3. MoisturizerLock in hydration with a comfortable, skin-appropriate moisturizer.
  4. SunscreenFinish with broad-spectrum SPF 30+. Reapply according to the label.
GLOWORA NOTE

Sunscreen is an important daily step. Use it generously and follow the product's instructions for reapplication.

WIND DOWN

Evening Routine

  1. CleanseRemove makeup, sunscreen, and the day's buildup with a suitable cleanser.
  2. Optional treatmentUse a treatment only as directed. Avoid layering several strong active ingredients until you know how your skin responds.
  3. MoisturizeFinish with moisturizer to help keep your skin hydrated overnight.
KEEP IT SIMPLE

More products do not always mean better results. A simple routine you can follow consistently is a great place to start.

KIND TO YOUR SKIN

Helpful Tips Before You Begin

Patch test new products. Follow the product instructions and stop use if irritation occurs.

Introduce products gradually. Add one new product at a time so you can notice how your skin responds.

Follow the label. Ingredients and usage directions vary by formula; do not assume every product is suitable for everyone.

Seek professional advice when needed. Persistent irritation or a skin condition is best discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
