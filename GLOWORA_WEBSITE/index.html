A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a little more clarity before you choose your routine?

How do I choose products for my routine?+

Start with your main skincare needs and keep your routine simple. Our Routine Guide explains the basic order: cleanse, treat, moisturize and protect during the day.

How does GLOWORA shipping work?+

After your order is confirmed, it is prepared, packed and handed to a courier partner for delivery within Pakistan. Available delivery options, estimated timing and charges are shown at checkout.

Can I use more than one skincare product together?+

It depends on the products and their ingredients. Introduce new products gradually, follow the label directions and patch test first. If you have a persistent skin concern, ask a qualified healthcare professional.

How can I share feedback about my experience?+

Use the “Write a Review” button in the Customer Reviews section to open the review form. Reviews submitted in this demo are saved in this browser only.

Where can I learn the correct order of skincare steps?+

Visit our Routine Guide for a beginner-friendly overview of morning and evening skincare steps.
